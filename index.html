Community-Led Research for Equitable Impact

The Horizon Insight Research Initiative (HiR) is a refugee-led, community-based research organization in Kakuma Refugee Camp, Kenya. We partner with communities to generate evidence that drives meaningful change.

About HiR

Mission

To empower displaced and host communities through participatory research, digital skill-building, and evidence-based advocacy, fostering equitable and sustainable solutions.

Vision

A world where refugee and host community voices are central in shaping the policies and programs that affect their lives.

Our Identity

HiR is refugee-led and community-based. Founded in 2025 and located in Kakuma 2, Turkana County, Kenya, we believe in research that is by the community, for the community.

Our Services

We offer a comprehensive suite of research and capacity-building services designed to support humanitarian and development actors while empowering local communities.

Community-Led Research

We design and implement ethical, participatory research that centers community voices and yields actionable insights.

  • Baseline & endline surveys
  • Focus groups & in-depth interviews
  • Needs assessments & vulnerability mapping
  • Social impact studies & program evaluation

Digital Training & Capacity Building

We equip youth and community researchers with practical digital skills for the modern evidence ecosystem.

  • Research methods & ethics training
  • KoboToolbox, Excel, Power BI, Canva
  • Data storytelling & visualization
  • Basic digital literacy & online safety

Data Services for Partners

We provide reliable, high-quality data services to NGOs, UN agencies, and academic institutions.

  • Data collection, cleaning & transcription
  • Quantitative & qualitative analysis
  • Dashboard development & data visualization
  • Report writing & summary briefs

Advocacy & Knowledge Sharing

We translate research findings into accessible formats to influence policy and practice.

  • Policy briefs & research dissemination
  • Youth-led community forums
  • Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) support
  • Awareness campaigns & community dialogues
